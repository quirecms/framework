Sixteen clubs, four groups and one weekend in April: this is how the spring cup's draw was made, what it means for each club, and what changes from last year.[^1]

## How the draw works

Clubs are drawn from four pots, by last season's ranking. No two clubs from the same town meet in a group, and the holders open on Friday evening.

> The draw is public, and every ball is shown to the camera before it's opened.

Each group plays a round robin. The two best clubs of each group go on to the quarter-finals on Sunday morning.

## What changes this year

- Matches are 2 × 20 minutes, with five minutes between halves.
- Each club may name 14 players, up from 12.
- Ties in a group are decided by goals scored, then by a penalty shoot-out.

The results are entered from the touchline and published as they come in:

```
GET /results?group=A&format=json
```
